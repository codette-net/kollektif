Pro-spektif

Pro-spektif is a new installation by Ger van der Zee, planned in June 2026. The work consists of 18 panels arranged in three circles, inspired by a future vision of a sustainable world in the year 2260.

The installation is intended to combine visual art with music, light projections, text, film, interviews, performances and other contributions from members of Kollektif and invited participants.

The aim of this work is to generate discussion and stimulate positive ideas for the future.
